## Projects

### 🌊 Technology Innovation Institute — Abu Dhabi

#### Unmanned Surface Vehicle (USV): From Remote Operation to Autonomous Supervision
_2023 - Present_

![Principal USV, the Detector, serves as the flagship for marine robotics research at the Technology Innovation Institute](/docs/detector.webp)

* Developed a 9-meter outboard-engine USV equipped with GNSS, INS, speed log, EO/IR, and LiDAR sensors for remote-controlled maritime operations, then advanced the platform to a 17-meter, 1,800 hp waterjet-propelled USV integrating GNSS, INS, LiDAR, radar, and EO/IR sensors into a unified autonomy stack.
* Advanced the platform from remote operation to autonomous supervision, enabling a single operator to oversee mission execution rather than directly pilot the vessel.
* Engineered perception and decision-making systems for reliable obstacle detection and safe navigation under high-level human supervision.

#### Large-Scale Container-Transport USV with International Partners
_2023 - Present_

![Hands-on ROV's ground control station at Oceaneering Stavanger, Norway](/docs/ground_control_station.webp)

* Supervising development, from the conceptual design phase, of a 17-meter LOA, catamaran-shaped, aluminium-hulled USV designed to navigate at sea while transporting a 20-foot container, in partnership with an international naval architect and shipbuilder.
* Followed the vessel's construction in China, with the first unit scheduled for delivery in 2027.
* Designed the sensor suite enabling full autonomy at sea in compliance with COLREGs (International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea).

#### Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV): Remotely Operated
_2025_

![Deployement of the 4-meter AUV, X301, in Underwater Test Pool](/docs/deploying_x301.webp)
![Remotely operating AUV](/docs/operating_x301.webp)

* Adapted a modified GraalTech X-300 AUV platform, integrating forward-looking sonar (FLS) and a front-facing camera for underwater perception and obstacle avoidance.
* Developed remote-operation control and monitoring capabilities to support subsea inspection and navigation missions.

### 🪖 ENSTA Paris, Polytechnic Institute of Paris — Paris

#### Challenge CoHoMa 2022: Multi-Robot Collaborative Reconnaissance
_2022_

![Ready to start the CoHoMa challenge reconnaissance mission](/docs/cohoma_challenge.webp)

* Designed and built a custom human-machine interface (HMI) from scratch, enabling a single operator to monitor and coordinate multiple robots (UGV, UAV) simultaneously in the field.
